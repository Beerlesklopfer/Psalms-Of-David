Psalm & David

David is a media server for the family: audiobooks, music, podcasts and radio on your own computer, with profiles, age limits and a weekly schedule for children. Psalm is the app for it on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Linux.

Important: Psalm is only the player. It needs a David server that your family runs itself. Without a server the app has nothing to play (the button "Try with demo data" only shows the screens).

What the app does

  • Listen to audiobooks and music from your own server, with your progress on every device
  • Download for listening without a network (offline mode with synchronisation)
  • Subscribe to podcasts, listen to radio, playlists
  • Labels and filters, search, profiles for children with an age limit
  • Pair by QR code or address with a one-time code
