David

David is the audiobook and music server of the family: it keeps the library, the profiles and the listening progress, and the apps (Psalm for iPhone, iPad, Android, Linux and Mac) play from it.

This installer puts the server on this Mac and sets it up as a daemon: a program that runs in the background, also when nobody is logged in, and starts again after a restart of the Mac (launchd). It runs under its own hidden account _david.

An update by a newer package keeps the data and the settings.
